GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Is Wazzup Messenger free?

Yes. Core group messaging is free. Premium is an account flag managed by the Leader in this version.

Can I create a group?

Yes. Create a group, invite existing Wazzup users by email, and manage your group members.

Does it update instantly?

Messages refresh automatically about every three seconds. Real-time WebSockets can be added later.
